# AI Fluency Interviews

AgentFluency helps employers assess how candidates use AI to complete realistic work across roles.

## Language

**AI fluency**: The ability to use AI effectively, verify its outputs, and exercise judgment while completing work.

**Interviewer**: The person who defines the work and configures the assessment environment.

**Candidate**: The person who joins through an interview invite and completes the assigned work.

**Interview session**: One candidate's attempt at an assessment, with its own isolated workspace.

**Workspace**: The environment in which the candidate receives the task, opens the provided files, and works with the AI assistant.

**Harness**: The interface and workflow a candidate uses to work with an AI model and its available tools.
